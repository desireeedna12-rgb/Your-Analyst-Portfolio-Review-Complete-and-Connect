Key findings and remaining gaps
- Week 1: The MR-17 incident involving the retired TEST_K44 command needs further investigation; the available evidence does not establish the cause or authorize operation.
- Week 2: The proposed eight-business-day QA review should be revised before approval because access, staffing, and the possible cost above the planning limit remain uncertain.
- Week 3: A night-shift operator's perspective and confirmation of the record custodian and authorizing sponsor are still needed.
- Week 4: The meaning of a held appointment must distinguish different operational situations; validated requirements are not proof of implementation.
- Week 5: The readiness decision and handoff between Scheduling and Clinical Operations require clarification.
- Week 6: The documented BRIDGE routing discrepancy remains unexplained. The editable data-flow diagram has flow/label inconsistencies that should be corrected to match the written report.
- Week 7: Eleven of twenty inspection records (55%) had at least one data-quality issue. Record R03 was flagged for missing information despite a READY status. Original records and responsible staff must verify corrections; a READY label is not evidence of machine safety.
